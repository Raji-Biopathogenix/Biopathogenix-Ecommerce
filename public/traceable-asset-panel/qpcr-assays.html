Lyophilized format

Dried, pre-formulated: reconstitute and run

Your assay's reagents, stabilized in dried form. Simpler setup, easier storage and a more forgiving supply chain.

  • Pre-formulated reagents: fewer pipetting steps, fewer setup errors
  • Less reliance on freezer space and cold-chain shipping
  • Consistent setup from reaction to reaction

FAQ

Custom assay Questions

Ask an assay specialist directly: Call (859) 444-5660 or email us at support@biopathogenix.com

Can my custom assay be lyophilized?

Yes. Every custom assay can be delivered as a wet (liquid) kit or a lyophilized kit. Tell us your preference in your request, or ask our scientists to recommend the best format for your lab.

What's the difference between wet and lyophilized formats?

Wet kits are liquid reagents stored at −20 °C and pipetted at setup. Lyophilized kits are dried, pre-formulated reagents you reconstitute and run, which simplifies setup and reduces reliance on freezer space and cold-chain shipping.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your target list, sample type, qPCR instrument and preferred format. Sequences, an existing protocol or performance goals help, but aren't required to start.

How many targets can go in one reaction?

Our assays include 8-target panels like the OctaPlex STI Assay. The right number for you depends on your instrument's channels and performance goals; we'll recommend it during scoping.

How long does a custom assay take?

A scientist reviews your request within 24 hours, and your written design plan includes the project timeline before you commit. Once your assay is live, reorders ship within 72 hours.

Who owns the assay design?

Ownership and usage terms are set out in your written design plan and quote, before you commit to anything. If you need specific terms, tell your design scientist during scoping.

Will my targets and data stay confidential?

Yes. We sign an NDA whenever a custom assay is in development, or any time you request one before sharing sequences or project details.
