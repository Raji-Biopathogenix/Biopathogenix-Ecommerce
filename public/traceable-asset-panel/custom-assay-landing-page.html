How It Works

Get Your Custom Assay Panel

STEP 01

Define Your Lab’s Needs

Tell us which targets, combinations, and format your lab needs. Our team can scope a custom single-target or multiplex configuration around your research requirements.

STEP 02

Develop the Assay

BioPathogenix engineers develop your custom assay using reference material from our 1,000+ strain biobank, then design the primers and probes, configure the reagents, and build the assay around your required targets and multiplex format.

STEP 03

Validate & Verify

We support analytical method validation and verification so your lab can establish that the assay performs as expected in its own environment.

STEP 04

Receive Your Custom assay Panel

Your custom assay panel is manufactured in the U.S. and shipped in 72 hours or less, with direct technical support available after delivery.
