Your Bench, Before and After

Six Setup Steps Become Two.

With a liquid master mix, every run starts with thawing, calculating, mixing and dispensing. Each step costs time and adds a chance for pipetting error. With a lyo plate, the reagents are already in the well.

Liquid master mix workflow

  1. Thaw enzyme, primers and probes on ice
  2. Calculate volumes and build the master mix
  3. Pipette master mix into every well
  4. Add sample
  5. Run the plate
  6. Refreeze or discard what's left over

BPX lyophilized plate

  1. Add sample
  2. Run the plate

No master mix to prepare and no reagent to distribute by hand. Anyone already comfortable setting up qPCR can run these plates without retraining.

FAQ

Questions Labs Ask Before Switching.

Answers from our R&D team. Ask us anything else when you request a quote.

What changes for my technicians day to day?

Very little. Technicians add the sample and run the plate. There is no reagent preparation step.

Do I need to add any other components?

No. The plates come pre-formulated with master mix, primers and probes. You add your template and proceed to PCR.

How many steps does reconstitution take?

One. The lyophilized reagent reconstitutes when you add your sample.

Will my staff need retraining?

No. Anyone familiar with setting up standard qPCR can run these plates.

Can I run a partial plate?

Yes. Our 96-well breakaway plates let you use only the wells you need for a given run.

What is the shelf life?

The plates have demonstrated 1-year stability and are designed to be stored and shipped at room temperature.

What sensitivity can I expect?

Sensitivity is 25 copies/µL.

Is there a minimum order?

No. There are no minimum order requirements, and we can also supply bulk quantities.

Can you make custom plates or panels?

Yes. Both the 96 and 384-well formats can be customized for multiplex real-time PCR, and we can provide bulk or custom plate formats as you scale.

Do you support validation?

Yes. We provide validation support to help with your lab's internal verification.

Can these be used for diagnostics?

No. BioPathogenix lyophilized plates are for Research Use Only and are not for use in diagnostic procedures.
